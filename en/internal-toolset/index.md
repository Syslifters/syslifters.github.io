# Internal Tools, Services and Providers

This is a list of our internal toolset that we use for running our company. Want to know something else? [Ask us](../contact-us/)!

Transition to European solutions

Due to international changes we are transitioning our entire infrastructure to European solutions and open-source alternatives for greater digital sovereignty. By the end of 2026 we plan to have fully migrated our core infrastructure (e.g. Authentik with ID Austria, Nextcloud, OnlyOffice, Fedora/Debian, Stalwart Mail Server on our own Proxmox cluster). **Therefore this toolset list is not up to date and will change in the coming weeks and months**.
